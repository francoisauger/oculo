Your videos, on the big screen.

Oculo is a free, open-source app for Android TV and Google TV. It plays YouTube in a calm interface made for the remote and readable from the couch.

View the code on GitHub Install: coming with version 1.0.

Pick up where you left off

The Continue watching row keeps what you started in the last 48 hours and did not finish. It is built from what is on the TV, with no network call.

Long-press a video and it stops showing up there.

The light follows the picture

The background takes the color of the video in focus, and a white ring breathes around it. You always know where you are, even from the back of the room.

Made for a remote

One press left opens the sections. One press up reaches search, by voice or by typing. The clock stays in its corner.

Sponsor segments skip themselves

SponsorBlock skips the segments its community has flagged. A small notice says so, then gets out of the way.

Nothing leaves the TV without a reason

No analytics, no crash reports. Even the sign-in QR code is made on the TV instead of by an outside service.

What Oculo talks to

Every network call in the code has been read, submodules included. This is where the app connects, when, and what goes there.

WhereWhenWhat goes there
YouTube and GoogleAlwaysBrowsing and playback requests, as with any YouTube app. If you sign in: your account, and your watch history unless you turn it off.
SponsorBlockAt each video, until you turn it offThe ID of the video. Read only: Oculo never votes.
Return YouTube DislikeAt each video, until you turn it offThe ID of the video.
Your TV’s speech serviceWhen you press the micYour voice, handled by the TV itself. Oculo only receives the text.
GitHubAt launch, to look for an updateNothing about you or your TV.
Analytics and crash reportsNeverThat code is not in the app.
QR code servicesNeverThe codes are drawn on the TV.

Off until you turn them on: DeArrow titles, the phone remote and Google Drive backup.

Where Oculo comes from

Oculo is based on SmartTube by yuliskov (MIT license). The playback engine is his, untouched. Oculo changes what you see: the home screen, the navigation, the focus and the colors.

All of it is public: the repository, the license and the list of every changed file.

Installing
